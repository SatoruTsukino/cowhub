# Plan 4.1: Golden Grass Server-Trust Probe (preview debug)

## Objective
The developer asked whether a client can force golden grass on the server. Golden Aura sets the
`Golden` attribute client-side only (FilteringEnabled → no replication). Log enough evidence to show
whether the server ever honours it: for every bite, record the node's client-visible golden state,
whether the hub spoofed it, and the full `GrazeResult` payload. Natural (game-originated) results are
logged too, as a baseline of what genuine golden rewards look like.
