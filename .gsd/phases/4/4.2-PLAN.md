## Protocol (from research)
- In: `ClientEvent.OnClientEvent("BotCheck", snap)` — `snap = {Phase, Id, Nonce, Stage, Prompt, Choices[{Id, Text, Tone, Icon}], Deadline, MovePrompt}`
- Phases: `QuickCheck` (3 rounds, 10 s deadline) → fail/expire → `RoomEntry` → `Dialogue` (3 stages) → `Released`
- Out: `BotCheckRequest:FireServer({Action, Id, Nonce, Stage, ChoiceId})`; Action = `Answer` | `Open` | `Dialogue`; resync `{Action = "Ready"}`
