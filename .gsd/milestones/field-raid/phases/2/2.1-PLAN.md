       - `tryChest` + `CloseChest` healing when hearts are low or chest is adjacent.
    4. **Challenge Orchestration (`tick`)**:
       - Pick next incomplete challenge from `challengesMask` (or user-selected mode: `Auto`, `C1: Ripe F3-4-5`, `C2: 3x Ripe F8`, `C3: Ogre Stomp + Clear`).
