# Plan 6.2: Dailies queue — auto rafting for Sly Moon + Furniture Store daily chest

## Objective
Turn dailies into a queue so Auto Dailies completes everything itself: Fisherman daily (auto-fish) runs
first; when it is not fishing, a Sly Moon daily that is Active starts a rafting voyage and stops it once
the quest is Ready, then claims it. Also open the Furniture Store daily gift chest once per day.
