# Plan 1.5: Natural mode: avoid water, sprint with stamina refill

## Objective
In Natural mode (walk, no teleports), the cow must never walk into water on its way to the caravan or back. It should sprint whenever stamina allows, walking to refill and then sprinting again, instead of sprinting until it hits 0.

## Context
- luascripts/preview.lua: CARAVAN RAID block (`caravan.walkTo`, `caravan.setSprint`) and `farm.isWaterAt(pos)` (downward ray hits Terrain `Water`).
- InputController `_setSprint`: it refuses at player attr `Stamina <= 0`. If the server attr `SprintActive == false` while the client wants to sprint, it re-sends `SprintRequest(true)` after 0.4 s.
- Config.Character: BaseSprintCapacity 4, BaseStaminaRegen 1/s, StaminaRegenDelay 0.5 s. Upgrades raise the capacity, so the max is tracked from the observed `Stamina`.

  <action>

    3. A goal (the intercept point or a stand point) that lands on water: walk to the guide-side point instead, or stay put if that is also water.
