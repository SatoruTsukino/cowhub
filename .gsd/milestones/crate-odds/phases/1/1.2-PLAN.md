# Plan 1.2: Team Chest Presets (Cave / Clearing, chest tier 1–4)

## Objective
Add "Team Chest – Cave" and "Team Chest – Clearing" to the preset dropdown. A chest-tier field (1–4, the
Fjord zone level) stays unlocked. The odds come from `TeamChestLoot` exactly as `TeamChestLoot.HatChance`
computes them: the per-tier hat weights, run through `CollectionLuckRules.Weights` with the whole hat collection as
the pity family, then multiplied by the chance that the chest drops a hat at all (`RareChance`).
