# Plan 1.9: Calories per Hour (best-field teleport, maturity and golden priority)

## Objective
Raise calories per hour without changing bite rate (already at the 0.4 s server minimum).
Game formula (Shared `FoodValue.Calculate`): `Category(zone).BaseValue × ZoneMultiplier × FoodModifiers (Golden ×2) × Maturity(0–1) × Rumen × …`.
- Category BaseValue: Fields1 7, Fields2 14, Fields3 21, Cave 28 (`FoodConfig`, `LiveBalance.Category`).
  - Fields1: HomeMeadow, CloverCreek, FishermansForest.
  - Fields2: SouthPastures, AppleOrchard.
  - Fields3: MountainsideMeadow, GoldenHill.
  - Zones missing from `FoodConfig.FieldGroups` (e.g. Fjord) fall back to Fields1.
- Plan 1.7 made the picker eat grass at `Available` (growth ≥ 0.5), so some bites land on partly grown grass.
- Golden grass (`Golden` attribute on the visual) is worth 2×.

## Context
