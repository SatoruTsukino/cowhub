# Plan 5.2: Fix Pre-Declaration Globals and CRLF Shell Scripts

## Objective
Close the two defects found during Plan 5.1.

1. `updateWallRaycastFilter` reads `cache`, and the `charConn` respawn handler reads `dodge`, `aerial` and
   `rafting`. All four are used before their `local` declarations, so Luau compiles them as globals, and the
   globals are nil. The respawn resets never ran. The wall filter never used `cache.grassFolder`.
2. `scripts/*.sh` have CRLF line endings, so `bash scripts/validate-all.sh` fails with a syntax error.

## Behavior change
Respawning or dying during an automated raft voyage now ends the session through `rafting.onSessionEnded`, as
`charConn` and the `Died` hook always intended. Dodge and aerial state also reset on respawn.
