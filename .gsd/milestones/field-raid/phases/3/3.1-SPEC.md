# SPEC.md — Standalone Field Raid Powerup XP Autofarm (`luascripts/field_raid_xp.lua`)

> **Status**: `FINALIZED` (2026-11-19)

## Vision
Build a self-contained standalone executor script (`luascripts/field_raid_xp.lua`) that maximizes **Field Raid Powerup XP/hour** using decompiled `FieldRaidPowerups`, `FieldRaidCatalog`, `FieldRaidEnemies`, `FieldRaidCombat`, and `FieldRaidLayout` mechanics, paired with a compact draggable HUD showing live equipped powerup levels, session XP, XP/hour, and mode toggles.

## Functional Requirements
- **REQ-01 Standalone Singleton Lifecycle:** Works without `cowhub.lua` loaded. Re-executing cleanly destroys any prior `_G.CowFieldRaidXP` instance and disconnects all connections. Starting pauses `_G.CowHubFieldRaid.auto` and `_G.CowMillQuests.running` to avoid `ChompRequest` chew-cooldown conflicts.
- **REQ-02 Live Powerup XP & Snapshot Sync:** Query `Remotes.GetSnapshot` on startup and after each raid (plus `ClientEvent("FieldRaid")` `Ended` payloads `ActionXP`, `ExitXP`, `EndXP`) to read `Profile.FieldRaids.Powerups` (`Equipped`, `XP`, `FieldRaidPowerups.Level`) and calculate real session XP gained and XP/hr.
- **REQ-03 Field 10 Rush & Target Scoring:**
  - Blitz Fields 1–9 forward (`-Z`) toward Field 10 (`Ogre’s Acre`), biting adjacent high-value targets/secondaries (such as `GoldMushroom` `110 XP` in F9) along the path.
  - Inside Field 10, score targets dynamically for maximum XP/sec:
    1. Secondary food (`GoldenApple` = `120 XP/bite`, `GoldMushroom` = `110 XP/bite`).
    2. Enemies weighted by `KillXP / remainingHealth` and distance (`Warlock` `150/34`, `VillageArcher` `75/23`, `FieldOgre` `500/160`, `Farmer` `40/14`, `VillageGuard` `70/30`, `CityGuard` `100/48`, `Spider` `20/8`).
    3. Field 10 `Wheat` (`31 XP/bite`) whenever in reach or between mob spawns.
- **REQ-04 Operating Modes:**
  - **`Rush & Reset` (default):** Blitz F10 -> eat `GoldenApple` + clear high-XP F10 pack (`FieldOgre`, `Warlock`, `VillageArcher`) until pack cleared and `estActionXP >= 500` -> clear via `RiverExit` ferry (`+1,000` Far Riverbank + `+500` Safe Exit + `OpenHarvestChest` + `HomePrompt`) -> auto-requeue solo raid.
  - **`Camp F10`:** Blitz F10 and hold within Field 10 band endlessly farming `GoldenApple`, continuous `Farmer` waves (`Rates.Farmer = 9`), fixed mobs, and respawning `Wheat`; triggers Safe Exit only on low Hearts (below 25% with no nearby chest) or manual `Bank & Leave`.
- **REQ-05 Telegraph Survival & Chest Healing:** Reuse `FieldRaidCombat.Contains` + `spotClear` blink-dodging (`blinkDodge`, `jink`, `jumpSilk`) and `OpenHarvestChest` / `CloseChest` healing.
- **REQ-06 Compact ScreenGui:** Draggable overlay displaying equipped powerup levels/progress, Session XP + XP/hr, run count, current field/hearts/mode, Start/Stop, Mode toggle (`Rush & Reset` / `Camp F10`), Bank & Leave, and Sync XP buttons.
- **REQ-07 Compile & Index Gates:** `luau-compile` 0 errors, max register `< 200`, and `FUNCTIONS.md` updated and verified with `scripts/update-functions-md.py --check`.

## Non-Goals
- Modifying `cowhub.lua`, `preview.lua`, or `mill_quests.lua`.
- Consuming finite player Raid Supplies (e.g. `WindCharge`, `PotOfLuck`) automatically without user action.
