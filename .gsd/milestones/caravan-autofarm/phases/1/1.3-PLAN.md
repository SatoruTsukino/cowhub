# Plan 1.3: Integrate Caravan Autofarm into preview.lua (staging)

> Depends on Plan 1.2 UAT. **Passed in run 5** (caravan_20261107_035154): Stopped from our moos solo, 3/3 crates, 75 loot from 79 bites, reveal closed, position restored.
> Port the proven logic from `luascripts/caravan_autofarm.lua`. The UAT-only probes stay in the standalone script: outgoing `__namecall` hook, expressions dump, convoy tree, miss_probe, Dump State.

## Objective
The caravan farm runs inside the hub. When the toggle is on and a caravan is active (OnRoad/Stopped), it **overrides every other autofarm**: the hub heartbeat pauses movement, grass chomp (Remote and Natural), mob aura, fishing, dodge, Manatite and Mithril. The town auction auto-bid doesn't teleport to the store. When the caravan leaves, the farm restores the position and the other farms resume where they were.

## Context
- .gsd/milestones/caravan-autofarm/MILESTONE.md (UAT runs 1–5)
- luascripts/caravan_autofarm.lua (proven logic)
- luascripts/preview.lua: `updateHeartbeatConnection` (single dispatcher, with the `farm.botCheckActive` gate), `chomp.fire` / `chomp.serverInterval` (already reads `CowBiteCooldown`), the GrazeResult handler (adaptive margin, ChewCooldown backoff), `chomp.release`, `isRewardDismissButton` / `activateGuiButton`, `setRootCFrame`, `townAuction.teleportToStore`

## Decisions
- **One shared gate:** `caravan.active` is checked once in the heartbeat, next to the bot-check gate. Grass `chomp.fire` refuses while it's set, so no other farm can bite.
  - On takeover: `chomp.release()` (Natural E), `dodge.cleanup()`, unanchor the root (Manatite/Mithril anchor it), and stop the humanoid.
- **Barrel bites go through the hub's ChompRequest path:** the shared serial, the `GrazeResult` margin and the ChewCooldown backoff. There is no second sender.
  - `chomp.fireBarrel` shares `chomp.fire`'s send core.
  - It bypasses only the grass-specific gates: mode = Remote, the caravan gate itself, and the grass skip reservation.
- **Rafting is not interrupted:** while a raft voyage is in progress, the caravan farm waits. Teleporting off a moving raft loses the voyage. Rafting has its own loop set that the heartbeat gate doesn't reach.
- The debug log goes to `cowhub/caravanlogs/` (phase, fear every 5 s, caravan events, graze rejects, kick results).
